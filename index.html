PRACTICE GUIDE

Make each answer
more convincing.

A short routine you can use before presenting your work or speaking with a teacher.

01

State your claim

Open with a clear position. Avoid spending your first sentence saying that the topic is “interesting”.

02

Give one concrete reason

Choose an example from your own work or experience. Specific evidence makes the answer memorable.

03

Consider another view

Show that you understand a possible objection, then explain where you stand.

04

Read it aloud

If a sentence is hard to say, it may be hard to follow. Shorten it and try again.
